# Newton–Kuramoto Physics (Deep Explanation)

## What this engine is
This is a single-oscillator physics model. It has:
- phase: where it is in its cycle
- omega: its natural frequency
- coupling: how much other oscillators would influence it (0 in safe mode)
- tick: the discrete time step counter

## What a step is
A step is one update of the oscillator’s phase using a small time increment (Δt).
The formula is:
phase_new = phase_old + (omega * Δt)

This is Newton-style stepping: small increments over time.

## What Kuramoto means
Kuramoto models synchronization between oscillators.
In the safe version, coupling = 0, so it behaves like a single free oscillator.

## How to step it
In PowerShell, type:
Tick

This:
- updates the phase
- increments tick
- prints the new state
- stops immediately
